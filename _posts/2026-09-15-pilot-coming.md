Greetings, loyal reader! I wanted to take a moment to mention that you are not forgotten. I've gotten busy with the site and currently have an intro set of exercises for "Data Analytics with Python!" Finally, if anyone is in Athens or Utrecht in October, swing by the respective PyCon. At both, I'll be giving my talk on what data models are, what they can do, and what they can't. I promise silly examples and enthusiasm. And who knows, maybe it'll even have facts, too! Have a great autumn, and let's get ready for a good launch!
