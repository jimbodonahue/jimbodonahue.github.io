Hi, I'm James. I woke up one morning and realized that there is a huge gap in data pedagogy, and I should fill it. That's my current project: (Jim's Data Gym)[www.jimsdatagym.com].

What qualifies me for that? Mostly, nobody else was doing it. But I was a full-time teacher for three years and have continued in a variety of volunteer and freelance positions over the years, including current positions with the ReDi School (data analytics) and the Sprachhafen (German). I also have a strong econometrics background, inlcuding a Masters of Science and two years of PhD coursework. This, combined with a bunch of self-learning about data science, machine learning, and Bayesian statistics, gives me the correct mix of empirical and didactic skills to hopefully pull this off.

In addition, I have also worked in: adventure tourism, nonprofit fundraising, hospitality, and economic research, on four different continents. These various posts have endowed me with a range of soft skills, from leadership to customer relations. On an average day, it means I have a bunch of random stories. On a good day, the skill set that set me up in a new country and new industry quickly means that I feel right at home in a startup environment. (And yes, on a bad day, it means I want to run away and join the circus.)

In my free time, I'm our garden's compost guru and child distracter, a mediocre acoustic guitar player, a reader of science fiction and other stories, and an occasional card player (Skat, anyone?). 

Feel free to reach out if you have thoughts, comments, critiques, questions, or suggestions! james@jimsdatagym.com
